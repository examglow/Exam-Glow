📋 Rules of Binomial Naming for IGCSE
  • Every organism is designated a two-part Latinized scientific name: Genus followed by species.
  • Genus always begins with a Capital letter (e.g., Homo, Panthera).
  • species always begins with a lowercase letter (e.g., sapiens, leo).
  • In print, binomial names must be written in italics; when handwritten in exam papers, they must be underlined.
