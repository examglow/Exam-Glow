The Four Main Arthropod Classes
Cambridge IGCSE Biology (0610 / 0970) • Diagnostic Features of Invertebrates
Universal Arthropod Features: 1. Exoskeleton made of chitin  •  2. Jointed legs  •  3. Segmented body  •  4. Bilateral symmetry
🐝
1. Insects
Legs:3 pairs (6 total)
Body parts:3 (Head, Thorax, Abdomen)
Antennae:1 pair
Wings:Usually 2 pairs (on thorax)
Bee, Butterfly, Beetle
🦀
2. Crustaceans
Legs:5+ pairs (10+ legs)
Body parts:Cephalothorax & Abdomen
Antennae:2 pairs
Breathing:Gills (Aquatic / Damp)
Crab, Lobster, Woodlouse
🕷️
3. Arachnids
Legs:4 pairs (8 total)
Body parts:Cephalothorax & Abdomen
Antennae:None (0 pairs)
Special:Chelicerae (fangs), Pedipalps
Spider, Scorpion, Tick
🐛
4. Myriapods
Legs:Many pairs
Body parts:Head + many similar segments
Antennae:1 pair
Difference:Centipede: 1 pair/seg
Millipede: 2 pairs/seg
Centipede, Millipede
