The Five Kingdoms of Living Organisms
Cambridge IGCSE Biology (0610 / 0970) • Core Diagnostic Features
🦁
1. Animals
Cells & Nucleus
Multicellular, Eukaryotic (True nucleus)
Cell Wall
Absent (Flexible cell membrane only)
Nutrition
Heterotrophic (Ingest other organisms)
Mammals, Insects, Fish
🌿
2. Plants
Cells & Nucleus
Multicellular, Eukaryotic
Cell Wall
Present (Made of Cellulose)
Nutrition
Autotrophic (Photosynthesis via Chloroplasts)
Ferns, Flowering Plants
🍄
3. Fungi
Cells & Nucleus
Multicellular (hyphae) or Unicellular (yeast)
Cell Wall
Present (Made of Chitin)
Nutrition
Saprotrophic (Secrete enzymes & absorb)
Mushrooms, Moulds, Yeast
🔬
4. Protoctists
Cells & Nucleus
Mostly Unicellular, Eukaryotic
Cell Wall
Variable (Some like plants, some animal-like)
Nutrition
Autotrophic (Chlorella) or Heterotrophic (Amoeba)
Amoeba, Paramecium
🧫
5. Prokaryotes
Cells & Nucleus
Unicellular, No true nucleus (Circular DNA)
Cell Wall
Present (Made of Peptidoglycan)
Organelles
No mitochondria, no chloroplasts, 70S ribosomes
Bacteria (E. coli, Lactobacillus)
